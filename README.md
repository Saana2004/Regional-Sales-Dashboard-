# 📊 Regional Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Regional Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using **Power BI and SQL** to analyze sales performance across different regions, states, counties, products, customers, and sales channels.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **revenue, profit, orders, and profit margins**, helping businesses identify sales trends, high-performing products, profitable regions, and key customers.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
* Track important business KPIs.
* Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
* Analyze product-wise sales and profitability.
* Compare performance across different sales channels.
* Identify high-performing regions, states, and counties.
* Analyze customer-level revenue contribution.
* Provide interactive insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, filtering, and analysis
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data storage and preprocessing
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI analysis

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following key performance indicators:

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Orders**
* **Profit Margin %**
* **Revenue per Order**
* **Monthly Revenue**
* **Monthly Profit**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview

* Overall revenue and profit performance
* Total orders and revenue per order
* Monthly revenue trends
* Monthly profit trends
* Monthly order trends
* Profitability analysis

### 2. Product & Channel Analysis

* Revenue by product
* Profit margin by product
* Product performance comparison
* Revenue by sales channel
* Profit by sales channel
* Profit margin by sales channel

### 3. Geographic & Customer Analysis

* Revenue by region
* Revenue by state
* Revenue by county
* Profit margin by county
* Revenue by customer
* Identification of high-performing geographic areas

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

* Products generating the highest revenue.
* Products generating the highest profit margins.
* Sales channels contributing the most revenue and profit.
* Regions and states with strong sales performance.
* Customers contributing significantly to total revenue.
* Monthly increases or decreases in revenue and profitability.
* Geographic areas that may require additional sales focus.
